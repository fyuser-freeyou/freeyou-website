Using these logos on invoices

Place the brand logo (e.g. digitFY) prominently at the top of the invoice header as the visual brand identity.
Below the brand logo, always include: FY Freelancing Solutions (OPC) Private Limited · GSTIN: 37AAGCF6483M1ZY · PAN: AAGCF6483M
In Zoho Books: Settings → Branding → Add Brand → upload the relevant logo + set the display name to the brand name. The legal entity details populate automatically from your org settings.
SAC code on invoice line items: 998314 for IT/freelance services (digitFY, buildFY) · 999299 for other professional services (contractFY, sourceFY, simplFY). Confirm exact codes with your CA.
